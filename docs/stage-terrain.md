# Dark stage terrain

Created with the built-in ImageGen tool. Saved as JPEG backgrounds in `apps/web/public/images/terrain/stage-1.jpg` through `stage-5.jpg`.
No buildings are baked into these textures. The game draws its real structures, contact shadows and projectiles separately.
Textures are additionally tinted with `0xb8b8b8` at runtime to keep attack effects prominent.

## Prompt set

Common production direction: portrait 2:3, strict top-down orthographic flat rectangular battlefield. Empty, continuous traversable ground. No buildings, towers, units, foundation pads, text, UI, roads or obstacles inside the play area. Quiet low-contrast matte center, soft diffuse lighting, mild edge vignette. Median brightness around 15–20%, highlights capped around 30%, no bright glow. Environmental decoration confined to the outermost 3%. Painted realistic sci-fi strategy texture designed for luminous projectile visibility.

1. **Stone Siege:** charcoal basalt paving mixed with dark gray-brown compact earth, broad fractured slabs, fine dust, dim teal crystal veins at rocky corners.
2. **Steel Line:** matte blue-gray gunmetal industrial panels, thin seams and light wear, recessed edge cables, faint faded hazard markings at corners.
3. **Swarm:** dark desaturated olive-gray soil, subdued organic residue and faint roots; chitin, muted amber egg sacs and moss only at edges.
4. **Descent:** ash-covered charcoal volcanic stone, warm reddish-gray weathered slabs, faint deep crimson cracks and tiny broken black ruins at edges. No lava rivers.
5. **Fold:** near-black indigo obsidian, large subtle angular tessellation and faint thin violet seams, small folded crystal formations at edges. No void holes or floating islands.
